About the Healthcare Industry

The healthcare industry is dedicated to providing medical services, developing new treatments, and improving patient health and quality of life. It includes hospitals, clinics, pharmaceutical companies, and medical technology organizations.

My Role in the Organization

I work as a Health Information Technician. My role involves managing electronic health records (EHRs), ensuring data accuracy, and protecting patient privacy. I also help healthcare professionals access and update patient data securely.

Ethical Issue in My Position

A major ethical issue in healthcare data management is maintaining patient confidentiality. It is unethical and illegal to share patient information without proper consent or authorization.

Cybersecurity Issues in Healthcare

1. **Data Breaches** – Hackers may target patient records to steal personal or financial information.
2. **Ransomware Attacks** – Cybercriminals can encrypt hospital systems and demand payment to restore access.

Cybersecurity Best Practices

- Use **strong passwords** and update them regularly.
- Enable **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** for all users.
- Train employees on **phishing awareness** and safe data handling.
- Regularly **update software and security patches**.
- Ensure **data encryption** for all patient records.
